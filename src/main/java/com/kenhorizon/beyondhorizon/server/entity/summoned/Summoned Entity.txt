Summoned Entity
Author: Ken Horizon
Base Foundation: Iron's n Spellbook


Summoned Entity have own damage source called pet damage this came with three types of damage: physical, magic and true damage, summon entity will always
stay at owner position, and they have no collision
Summoned Entity will attack neareast hostile entity in the range of player, each summoned entity have unique attack range, summoned entity will priotize
the targeted entity by the owner

Summoned Entity will not despawn until the owner is killed or left the game, the owner have fix of 3 slots of summoned entity and can add by one or two
by using items and potions

The summoned item can be used as priotize target by looking at entity and RMB all summoned entity will target the locked target even the other summoned
unit not came from the item will also attack the locked target
