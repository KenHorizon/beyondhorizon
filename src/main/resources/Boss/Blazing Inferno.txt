Blazing Inferno
HP: 400
Armor: 24
Damage Reduction: 15%
Damage: 16

Summoning:
Blazing inferno can be spawn by using flame cell on unpowered blazing inferno on the ruin flames
the blazing inferno can be resummoned after defeating by using defenders of core flame

Attacks:

Blazing Rods - Blazing inferno shoot 3 blaze rod projectiles that deal 1 + 2% target's current health magic damage
	if enraged mode it deal 2 instead of 1 if Blazing inferno health drop on below 25% it started shooting 
	from 3-10 projectiles

Blazing Spears - Blazing inferno summon spears that home to target after shorts delay blazing inferno have 3 form of
	attacks, first shoot 3 at same time, second shoot 3 at same time with no delay, and last is shoot one spear
	every seconds

Dash - Blazing inferno it idle for 2 seconds and later it dash to target dealing 25% total of attack damage
	if blazing inferno on enraged mode it dash 3 times, at the end of dash on third times it will do a weaker
	shockwave attack that deal no damage

Shockwave - Blazing inferno charge upto 4 seconds and later will do a shockwave dealing 5% total of attack damage +
	1% target's max health and knocking back and disabling shield for 5 seconds if blazing inferno is enraged it
 	deal 2% target's max health instead of 1%

Ground Slam - Blazing inferno charge upto 3 seconds and jump on air and slamming within 12 radius on ground dealing 
	5% total of attack damage + 11% target's max health and knocking up if blazing inferno enraged it will leap
	to current target

Death Ray - Blazing inferno will perform charge upto 5 seconds and it will release powerful death ray that deal
	1 + 2% target's current health magic damage every 0.01 seconds

Eruption - Blazing inferno will perform shockwave attack and release a bursting magma that deal 16 magic damage and
	burn the target for 3 seconds

Behavoir:
The blazing inferno has two phases during first phase all attack are not empowered and mostly follow the current target
when the blazing inferno drop on below 50% health it will on phase of enraged phase that become immune to all damage
for 5 seconds and after the phase ended will immedietly perform a ultimate attack (Death Ray) and all attack are 
empowered during all phases

Drop:
Wildfire fragments
Heart of the inferno
